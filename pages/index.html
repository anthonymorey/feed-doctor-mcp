Before you connect

Can FeedDoctor change my merchant account?

No. Google does not provide a read-only Merchant scope, so its consent screen may mention broader access. FeedDoctor uses GET/list calls only. When Microsoft support opens, it will also use Microsoft’s merchant management scope for reading data only.

Where are my tokens stored?

Access and refresh tokens are encrypted inside the bearer token you hold. We have no token database. Requests are processed in memory. Never share your bearer token or configuration publicly.

Does it fix products automatically?

No. Diagnostics are rules-based. You review the suggestions and make changes in your own tools. Results do not guarantee approval.

How long does my connection last?

Up to 30 days. Access tokens refresh during requests when the provider permits it. Reconnect after expiry or provider revocation.

Which clients work?

Clients supporting Streamable HTTP with bearer authentication can connect directly. A Node.js bridge configuration is also shown for Claude Desktop versions requiring local server configuration.

Can I use the merchant dashboards instead?

Yes. Merchant Center dashboards remain the source of truth. FeedDoctor is for people who want the diagnostics available through their MCP workflow.
